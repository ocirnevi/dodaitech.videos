## Descripción corta
IBM cools its quantum chips to a fraction of a degree above absolute zero, colder than outer space. Why? Qubits are incredibly sensitive: even tiny amounts of heat disturb their quantum states and cause errors. At around −273 °C, the circuits stay quantum long enough to compute.

Sources: IBM Quantum (dilution refrigerators, ~15 mK); Fixsen, ApJ 2009 (cosmic background 2.7 K).
