## Descripción larga (alternativa)
What if you could remember every conversation you've ever had? Not with your brain. With AI.

A new wave of AI memory wearables like Omi and Plaud clip to your shirt, wrist or neck, record what's said around you, turn it into text and let you ask your "memory" anything later: what your boss asked you to do, that idea you had over coffee. Some are designed to capture conversations all day long.

Sounds incredible. But your conversations aren't just yours. They involve friends, coworkers and complete strangers. So if AI could remember your entire life… would you actually want it to?

Tell me in the comments: would you wear one?
