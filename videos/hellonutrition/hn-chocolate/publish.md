## Descripción corta
Cocoa contains flavanols that can help maintain healthy blood flow and blood vessels, but many chocolate bars are mostly added sugar with little cocoa. Choose 70%+ dark chocolate, check the added sugar, keep portions small, and remember that processing (alkalization) can strip flavanols.

Sources: EFSA Journal 2012;10(7):2809; Hooper et al., Am J Clin Nutr 2012; Miller et al., J Agric Food Chem 2008.
